Our process

Every matter follows clearly defined stages.

01of 05 stages
  • Initial consultation
  • Case assessment
  • Preparation
  • Representation
  • Resolution and follow-up
01 / 05

Initial consultation

We listen first. You explain your situation, your goals and your deadlines, and we tell you honestly what is realistic.

Scope
  • A confidential first meeting
  • Clarifying your objectives
  • A first view of your options
  • Explanation of fees and next steps
02 / 05

Case assessment

We study the documents and the law, weigh the strengths and the risks, and agree a clear strategy with you.

Scope
  • Review of documents and evidence
  • Legal analysis
  • Risk assessment
  • A written strategy
03 / 05

Preparation

Everything is prepared before any step is taken: the drafting, the evidence and the formal correspondence.

Scope
  • Drafting of agreements and pleadings
  • Preparation of evidence and witnesses
  • Formal notices and letters
  • A negotiation plan
04 / 05

Representation

We act for you through negotiation, mediation, or before the courts and arbitral tribunals, whichever serves you best.

Scope
  • Negotiation
  • Mediation
  • Court hearings
  • International arbitration
05 / 05

Resolution and follow-up

We secure the outcome, enforce it where needed, and make sure you know exactly what comes next.

Scope
  • Settlement or judgment
  • Enforcement
  • Appeals where appropriate
  • Closing advice
